# Policy 2.2 behavior evidence

Issue #174 extends the existing card-aware strategy-v2; it does not introduce a solver. Exact stored 2.0.0 and 2.1.0 configurations roll forward to 2.2.0, with old raw actions/versions retained. Wrong digests remain errors. Mirror keeps its observed model and shared helper formulas; no new bluff gating is imposed on observed mirror distributions.

The existing preflop strength position bonus remains the single participation adjustment (no duplicate bonus). All value and eligible bluff raises share canonical and 1.2x target sizes, rounded to the small blind and clamped/merged by legality. Position changes their conditional mixture: canonical weight UTG 0.80, CO 0.65, BTN 0.60. This is not a solved balance ratio.

Non-value postflop raises require a current public heads-up line: previous-street initiative without a current bet against us, or an in-position checked-to flop/check-through probe. Unknown history/position, current-street folds from a formerly multiway pot, multiway pots, all-in opponents and bets against us have zero bluff mass. Draw bluff mass is capped at persona bluff trait times 0.35; other weak-hand mass at times 0.15. Removed mass moves to legal passive continuation (check/call), deliberately retaining the base participation rate rather than inventing a new fold model. Value and bluff reasons retain the public line and canonical sizing rule; the explicit amount is authoritative for the mixed larger target and legal clamps.

`node tools/benchmark-policies.js --assert --json` reports 18 position/persona cells with 10,000 fixed-seed draws each, fixed 0.02 absolute-frequency tolerance, and a 24-cell public-line grid. The 0.010611 maximum observed frequency error checks sampler consistency with declared distributions, not independently observed policy quality. Position-order and public-line/size-support assertions are the policy behavior gates. The eight sizing rows use made-value postflop fixtures to exercise every size rather than passing on zero raises. Persona participation can tie at its 0.98 cap while its size distribution still differs by position. Timing is development-machine wall time, not a stable performance guarantee.

Legality, deterministic/hidden-state invariance, all-in/min-raise checks, exact predecessor recovery, and the existing 6-max mirror nearest-persona calibration passed. These are structural/distribution tests, not proof of poker strength, profit, GTO accuracy or user learning.
